# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Nextbook ("we", "us") collects when you
use the Nextbook platform ("the Service"), why we collect it, and what rights
you have over it.

Two different relationships are covered here:

- Where **your employer or organisation** has a Nextbook account, they decide
  what business data goes into the Service. They are the **controller** of that
  data; we act as their **processor** and handle it on their instructions.
- For your own **account and login information**, we are the controller.

## 1. What we collect

**Account data.** Your name, email address, hashed password, preferred language
and calendar, interface preferences, two-factor authentication settings, and
the roles and permissions assigned to you.

**Business data you enter.** Records you create in the Service — items,
inventory movements, customers, suppliers, purchases, sales, expenses, journal
entries, payments and receipts, and any contact details contained within them.

**Activity and audit data.** Because the Service is an accounting system, it
keeps an audit trail: which user created, updated or deleted a record, and
when. This is a deliberate feature and cannot be switched off for financial
records.

**Technical data.** IP address, browser and device type, timestamps, and error
diagnostics — collected in server and application logs to keep the Service
secure and working.

**Cookies.** We use strictly necessary cookies only: a session cookie to keep
you signed in, and a CSRF token cookie to protect form submissions. We do not
use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 2. Why we use it

| Purpose | Basis |
|---|---|
| Providing the Service and its features | Performance of a contract |
| Authenticating you and securing accounts | Legitimate interest, legal obligation |
| Maintaining the financial audit trail | Legal obligation, legitimate interest |
| Diagnosing faults and improving reliability | Legitimate interest |
| Responding to your support requests | Performance of a contract |
| Sending service notices (e.g. security, downtime) | Legitimate interest |

We do **not** sell personal data, and we do not use your business data to train
machine-learning models.

## 3. Who we share it with

- **Within your organisation.** Other users of your company account can see
  records according to the roles and permissions your administrator sets,
  including the audit trail showing your name against records you changed.
- **Service providers.** Hosting, database, backup and error-monitoring
  providers who process data on our behalf under written terms, and only as
  needed to run the Service.
- **Legal.** Where we are required to disclose by law, court order, or to
  protect our rights or someone's safety. Where we are permitted to tell you,
  we will.
- **Business transfer.** If Nextbook is acquired or merged, data may transfer as
  part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

## 4. Where data is held

Data is stored on servers operated by our hosting providers. Where data is
transferred outside your country, we rely on appropriate safeguards with the
provider concerned.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Business and accounting records** are retained for as long as your
  organisation's account is active. Deleted records are soft-deleted and remain
  recoverable by your administrators until permanently purged, so that
  accidental deletions and the audit trail can be reconciled.
- **After account termination**, data is retained for **30 days** to allow an
  export, then deleted from active systems. Backups age out on their normal
  rotation.
- **Technical logs** are typically retained for up to 90 days.
- We may retain records longer where a law — such as statutory bookkeeping
  retention — requires it.

## 6. Security

We protect data with encryption in transit (TLS), password hashing, role- and
permission-based access control, per-company data separation, optional
two-factor authentication, and an audit trail on financial records. No system
is perfectly secure, but we work to keep the risk low, and we will notify
affected customers and any relevant authority of a personal-data breach as
required by law.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete,
export, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to
processing based on legitimate interests.

Much of this you can do yourself: your profile page lets you review and correct
your account details, and your preferences are yours to change at any time.

For business data held on behalf of your organisation, please contact your
Nextbook administrator first — we will refer such requests to them, because
they control that data. For anything else, contact us using the details below
and we will respond within the time the applicable law allows.

## 8. Children

The Service is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not
knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Where a change is material we will give notice
before it takes effect, and the effective date at the top of this page will be
updated.

## 10. Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to **privacy@nextbook.af**.
